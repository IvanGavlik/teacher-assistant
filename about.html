About Teacher Assistant

I built Teacher Assistant because teachers shouldn't spend their evenings making slides.

Teacher Assistant started with a simple observation: too much of a teacher's time goes into preparing materials instead of teaching.

Read the story ↓
IG

Ivan Gavlik · Founder, Teacher Assistant

I kept seeing the same problem.

Teachers were spending evenings creating vocabulary lists, exercises, presentations and other materials — work that was important, but repetitive.

At the same time, AI was becoming capable of generating exactly this kind of content.

The problem wasn't whether AI could help. The problem was putting it into a workflow teachers would actually want to use.

That's why I started building Teacher Assistant.

The idea

A workflow teachers would actually want to use.

Old workflow

Idea → Google → Word → PowerPoint → Copy/Paste → Formatting → More searching → Finally teach

Teacher Assistant workflow

Idea → Teacher Assistant → Review → Edit → Teach

AI should remove the repetitive work — not remove the teacher.

What we're building

A better way to prepare language lessons.

Teacher Assistant is an AI-powered lesson preparation tool built specifically for language teachers.

Generate lesson materials

Turn a simple lesson idea into structured teaching materials.

Adapt to CEFR levels

Create content appropriate for A1 · A2 · B1 · B2 · C1 · C2.

Vocabulary, grammar & more

Vocabulary, grammar, reading and exercises — built to fit the lesson.

Works inside PowerPoint

Generates directly inside the presentation workflow you already use.

I'm a software developer who likes building products that remove unnecessary complexity. I've spent more than a decade building software across fintech, e-commerce, and enterprise systems.

Teacher Assistant started from a simpler idea: if technology can save a teacher several hours every week, that time is worth giving back.

wortkraftmaster.org GitHub dev.to
An honest question

I'm a developer, not a teacher. That's why teachers are part of the process.

Teacher Assistant isn't built from assumptions about what teachers need. The product is shaped through conversations, feedback and real usage from language teachers. The goal isn't to tell teachers how they should teach — it's to listen to what takes their time and build tools that make that work easier.

What we believe

Our principles

Teachers stay in control

Teacher Assistant generates. The teacher decides.

Useful beats impressive

We don't care how clever an AI feature sounds if it doesn't solve a real classroom problem.

Simple by default

A teacher shouldn't need a tutorial to create a lesson.

Quality matters

Teaching materials need to be appropriate, understandable and useful — not just generated quickly.

Work in progress, on purpose

We're building Teacher Assistant with teachers, not just for them.

The best product ideas don't come from a boardroom — they come from the people using the product. I'm continuously collecting feedback from language teachers about what takes the most time, what they actually need, and what AI gets wrong.
