Frequently asked questions

Teacher Assistant is an AI-powered PowerPoint add-in that helps language teachers create lesson materials faster. Describe the lesson you want to teach, and it generates ready-to-use slides — vocabulary, grammar, reading, speaking activities and more — directly inside your presentation.

Teacher Assistant works with the language you describe in your request. It's most commonly used for English, Spanish, French, German and Italian lessons, and can generate materials in other languages too.

Teacher Assistant generates content for every CEFR level, from A1 (beginner) to C2 (proficient).

Yes. Teacher Assistant is a Microsoft PowerPoint add-in. It runs as a sidebar inside PowerPoint (desktop and web) and inserts generated slides directly into your open presentation, matching your deck's theme and fonts.
